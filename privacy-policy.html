🌾 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Welcome to Harvesting Happily. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you register, we store your username and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account ID and email address.
  • Game Data: We store your in-game progress, including farm state, inventory, coins, levels, and achievements.
  • Session Data: We create authentication tokens to keep you logged in securely.
  • Push Notification Subscriptions: If you opt in, we store a subscription token to send you game-related notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the game service
  • Save and restore your game progress across sessions
  • Send optional push notifications about game events (only if enabled)
  • Authenticate you and protect your account

3. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google:

  • We receive your Google account ID and email address from Google
  • We do not receive or store your Google password
  • You can unlink your Google account at any time from the game settings
  • Your Google data is used only for authentication and account linking

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard PBKDF2 hashing. We use session tokens with expiration and inactivity timeouts to protect your account.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google's OAuth 2.0 service for authentication. Google's use of your information is governed by their Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your account information
  • Unlink your Google account from the game settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the game's support channels.
